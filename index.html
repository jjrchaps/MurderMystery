Agent Evelyn “Iron-Badge” Vance

The Crooked Prohibition Agent
“Deadpan authority, corrupt G-woman, razor-sharp hypocrite, looks down her nose at the boys.”
🍸 Public Persona

The former poster girl for the Bureau of Prohibition who discovered that protecting speakeasies pays much better than raiding them. She sips confiscated Scotch at the end of the mahogany bar while keeping everyone in line with cold civil-service bureaucracy and a raised eyebrow.

🎩 Costume & Prop Ideas

Tailored 1920s skirt suit or high-waisted wide-leg trousers, trench coat draped over shoulders, fedora or cloche hat, shiny brass or silver badge on lapel, and a small leather notebook with a fountain pen.

Acting Cue & Quip Treat every gangster in the room like an unruly schoolboy. Drop dry legal jargon and remind people that “everything in this city has an administrative fee.”

Daisy “Duchess” Wellington

The Heavyweight Boxing Promoter
“Cigar-chomping, swaggering queen of the fight game; bobs, weaves, and hustles.”
🍸 Public Persona

Fearless, loud manager who clawed her way up from the stockyards to become the premier boxing promoter in Chicago. Always waving ringside tickets, bragging about her heavyweight contenders, and pacing like she is in the 15th round of a title fight.

🎩 Costume & Prop Ideas

Sharp tailored pant-suit or tweed skirt with suspenders and vest, fedora or newsboy cap tilted over one eye, stopwatch on a neck cord, unlit cigar or cigarette prop, and a rolled-up sports newspaper.

Acting Cue & Quip Fast, punchy energy! Shadowbox or tap people playfully on the shoulder. Use boxing metaphors for everything: “Keep your chin tucked, kid, or you won't make the bell!”

Alderman Cornelia “Greasy Palm” Higgins

The Corrupt City Alderman & Ward Boss
“Grand political matriarch of the 19th Ward: Theatrical orator, adjusts her pearls, blusters with mock indignation.”
🍸 Public Persona

The grand dame of City Hall running for Mayor on the “Reform Ticket.” She preaches civic virtue and temperance by day, and drinks the speakeasy dry by night while holding court, kissing cheeks, and promising city contracts to anyone who votes right.

🎩 Costume & Prop Ideas

Glamorous evening coat with faux-fur collar or feather boa, silk dress, layered long pearl necklaces, feather cloche hat, vintage campaign pin (e.g. “Higgins For Mayor”), long cigarette holder, and satin pumps.

Acting Cue & Quip Dramatic and grand! Address everyone like your beloved constituents. Deliver theatrical toasts and gasp with royal outrage if anyone questions your unimpeachable integrity.

The Velvet Blind Head Bartender

Acting Cue & Quip Unflappable confidence. Slide imaginary cocktails, roll your eyes at gangsters' dramatic excuses, and dish out devastating one-liners: “Spill your drink or spill your story, pal—make up your mind.”

Bernice “Bernie” Rothstein

The High-Stakes Backroom Bookie
“Human calculating machine: Rapid-fire banter, nervous fidgety energy, parlay odds genius.”
🍸 Public Persona

A math prodigy who runs the sports book and backroom numbers racket. Quotes betting odds on literally everything that happens in the room, furiously scribbles on scraps of paper, clicks her fountain pens, and never sits completely still.

🎩 Costume & Prop Ideas

Green celluloid dealer's eyeshade or round wire spectacles, pinstripe vest over silk blouse, sleeve garters, pockets full of pens, racing forms, betting slips, and a notebook full of rapid numbers.

Acting Cue & Quip Fast, fidgety math brain! Quote immediate odds on whatever someone says: “Three-to-one says that's a lie, seven-to-two says you're bluffing, pal!” Scribble frantically on napkins.

Salvatore “Sal” Torrio

The Rival North-Side Mob Boss
“The Cicero Overlord: Aristocratic mob royalty, chillingly polite, speaks in a purring whisper, never raises his voice.”

Deadly calm, impeccably tailored syndicate boss in pearl-gray spats and a white carnation. Never raises his voice—when Sal speaks, the entire speakeasy leans in to hear him. Exudes old-world authority, polite menace, and dangerous charm.

🎩 Costume & Prop Ideas

Tailored dark 3-piece suit, contrasting vest, white carnation lapel pin, diamond pinky ring, white spats over polished dress shoes, and a classic fedora.

Acting Cue & Quip Quiet, chilling elegance. Never shout. Speak softly with razor precision. Flick invisible dust off people's lapels and smile politely: “In this city, manners are the only thing separating us from the animals.”

Doc “Sawbones” Blanchard

The Disgraced Back-Alley Doctor
“Cultured Southern drawl, cynical humor, clinks when walking, takes people's pulses mid-conversation.”
🍸 Public Persona

A former surgeon who lost his medical license and now patches up gangsters who cannot visit Cook County Hospital. Always three drinks ahead of the room, taking pulses without asking, and carrying a leather doctor's bag that clinks with amber bottles.

🎩 Costume & Prop Ideas

Rumpled suit or dark vest, round wire spectacles or pince-nez, stethoscope or vintage leather doctor's bag, pocket penlight, and amber glass medicine bottles.

Acting Cue & Quip Slow, cynical Southern drawl. Grab someone's wrist to take their pulse mid-sentence, clink bottles in your doctor bag, and diagnose people: “Your pulse says guilt, but your liver says another bourbon.”

🍸 What You Need to Know for Game Night

🎭 Do I need to memorize lines?

Not at all! There are no scripts. You just arrive in your outfit, talk to each other in character, and mingle. The game provides everything you need on your phone.

🔑 When do I get my secrets?

Your confidential game dossier, private goals, and murder secrets are securely locked. You will receive a private PIN to unlock your file on your phone when you arrive!

👗 What if I don't have a vintage costume?

Don't worry! Everyday clothes work great with one or two simple accessories: suspenders, a vest, a fedora, a string of pearls, slicked-back hair, or a simple hat.

🍸 How does the game play?

You'll mingle, exchange rumors, discover clues, inspect evidence cards, and try to piece together who killed Big Louie before the final vote!
